SHARED POTENTIAL
SPARCS and UCI both describe hospital encounters and contain age groups,
gender, race, admission categories, discharge disposition, diagnoses, and
length of stay. SPARCS adds facility/geographic detail and charges/costs.
This makes SPARCS a candidate for expanded encounter and resource analysis,
subject to cohort selection and semantic harmonization.

COMPATIBILITY LIMITS TO RESOLVE BEFORE SELECTION
- Neither SPARCS candidate's published field list contains a readmission
  outcome or patient linkage identifier. They cannot directly increase
  labeled readmission training volume or support inferred patient matching.
- UCI and SPARCS cover different periods and populations. Do not present
  pooled rates as like-for-like comparisons without cohort restrictions.
- UCI uses ICD-9 diagnosis fields; these SPARCS files publish CCSR diagnosis
  categories. Diagnosis integration needs a documented coarse grouping or
  separate coding-system dimensions, not equality of raw codes.
- Age bands differ. Establish a valid shared representation or keep the
  original source bands, rather than imply exact ages are known.
- Investigate the precise length-of-stay conventions and top-coding before
  combining bed-day totals or filtering comparable encounters.
- 2023 and 2024 have schema name differences, including service-area and ZIP
  fields. Normalize documented meanings and validate each file separately.
- Hospital identifiers in SPARCS are not available as identified hospital
  keys in the UCI data; do not assign UCI encounters to SPARCS facilities.
- Only a field-level/sample review has been completed. No merged volume,
  validation result, model improvement, or completed warehouse is claimed.

PROVISIONAL MODELING OPTION
An integrated warehouse could support common encounter analysis, with
source-specific facts for readmission outcomes and hospital costs. The
readmission model would use legitimately labeled encounters; expansion
records could support broader clustering and operational analysis where
their fields are suitable. This remains an option, not a locked design.
