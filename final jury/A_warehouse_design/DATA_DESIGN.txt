BUSINESS PROBLEM
Extend the existing hospital/readmission project with a larger, real-data
warehouse supporting two linked management questions:
1. Within the labeled diabetes cohort, which discharge profiles are associated
   with subsequent readmission and merit follow-up review in a demonstration?
2. Across the expanded hospital discharge data, which case-mix groups consume
   more inpatient stay and estimated cost, and how do facilities differ within
   each source year?

Intended users: hospital analytics staff and care-management planners.
Outputs planned for later blocks: readmission classifier comparison, patient
or encounter segmentation, association rules, and source-filtered resource
analysis. These are academic analyses, not validated clinical recommendations.
No reduction in readmissions, cost savings, or live occupancy forecast is claimed.

INTEGRATION STRATEGY
Use a schema-aligned UNION of encounter sources, not a patient-to-patient
join. Retain original source snapshots and all fields in compressed raw CSV
pages; write a typed common Parquet contract for Snowflake staging.

Grain: one published hospital encounter/discharge record per source snapshot.
Key: source_key + ':' + source_row_id. UCI uses its encounter_id; SPARCS uses
the publisher platform's record ID (:id) for snapshot record traceability.
The platform ID is not a patient identifier or a longitudinal admission ID.
Keep source-specific patient keys only where the source provides them.
The two new years do not overlap the UCI observation period.

IMPLEMENTED FACTS AND DIMENSIONS FOR BLOCK A
The deployed design is a star schema with FACT_ENCOUNTER and seven dimensions.
It supersedes the earlier proposed separate readmission/cost facts. Nullable
source-specific measures and labels are retained in the common fact table.

FACT_ENCOUNTER primary key: ENCOUNTER_KEY (one source-qualified record).
Seven numeric surrogate foreign keys reference the dimensions below.
DIM_SOURCE: SOURCE_KEY, DIABETES_COHORT_DEFINITION.
DIM_PERIOD: PERIOD, DISCHARGE_YEAR.
DIM_DEMOGRAPHIC: SOURCE_KEY, AGE_BAND_RAW, AGE_BAND_COMMON, GENDER, RACE_RAW, ETHNICITY_RAW.
DIM_ADMISSION: SOURCE_KEY, ADMISSION_TYPE_RAW, ADMISSION_TYPE_COMMON, DISPOSITION_RAW, PAYMENT_TYPE, UCI_ADMISSION_SOURCE_ID.
DIM_DIAGNOSIS: SOURCE_KEY, DIAGNOSIS_SYSTEM, DIAGNOSIS_CODE, DIAGNOSIS_DESCRIPTION, APR_DRG_CODE.
DIM_FACILITY: SOURCE_KEY, FACILITY_KEY, FACILITY_NAME, HOSPITAL_COUNTY, SERVICE_AREA.
DIM_TREATMENT: SOURCE_KEY, UCI_INSULIN, UCI_CHANGE, UCI_DIABETESMED, UCI_A1CRESULT, UCI_MAX_GLU_SERUM.

All 51 source fields are reconstructed by V_ENCOUNTER_ANALYTICS. Null-safe
joins retain unknown/redacted fields without dropping or inventing records.
The typed ETL snapshot precedes dimensional loading. V_WAREHOUSE_CHECKS
checks counts, unique keys, orphan keys, source boundaries and type conversions.
The user confirmed Snowflake PASS, 4,424,257 fact rows, seven dimensions and
zero failed checks. V_SOURCE_SUMMARY and 07_demo_queries.sql support analysis.

HARMONIZATION
- Preserve raw source fields and missingness. Never replace unknown targets
  with NO, and never match unrelated patients across sources.
- Map UCI's decade age bands and SPARCS bands to 0-29, 30-49, 50-69, 70+.
  This coarser representation is exact for the reported bands; no age or
  age midpoint is invented.
  The 2024 records use hyphenated labels (e.g. 50-69), while 2023 uses
  wording such as 50 to 69. Both documented representations are mapped.
- Normalize M/F to Male/Female; keep unknown/missing values visible.
- Map admission IDs using the official UCI IDS_mapping.csv, not numeric
  proximity. Retain original and common categories.
- Keep ICD-9-CM and CCSR ICD-10-CM as distinct diagnosis systems. Do not join
  matching-looking codes or pretend that an exact crosswalk was performed.
- Preserve source periods. UCI has no encounter dates or discharge year;
  these remain null. No trend line or future readmission interval is inferred.
- SPARCS 120+ length-of-stay records are lower bounds, not exact 120-day stays.
  Mark them explicitly. The SPARCS dictionary defines LOS using discharge
  minus admission plus one, excluding leave-of-absence days. UCI documents
  time_in_hospital differently and restricts stays to 1-14 days. Therefore,
  compare LOS within source, and do not report a pooled stay estimate as a
  like-for-like clinical comparison.
- Parse dollar/comma formatting for charges and estimated costs. Zero is
  retained as zero; missing remains null. Negative values are retained and
  counted for review, not silently discarded. The raw financial strings are
  authoritative. Parquet numeric values use float64; aggregate presentation
  should round to cents or cast to fixed decimal in Snowflake.
- SPARCS estimated cost uses facility cost-to-charge ratios, not observed
  patient payments, insurer reimbursement, or demonstrated savings. The 2023
  overview describes 2019 audited ratios used for its 2023 cost estimates.
  The 2024 overview uses 2021 audited ratios. Disclose this difference when
  comparing estimated costs between the two years.
- Redacted/missing facilities and demographics stay unknown. Hospital-level
  analyses must report their coverage rather than reassign these records.

MODELING BOUNDARIES FOR LATER BLOCKS
Classification: UCI labeled encounters only until another source with an
equivalent observed outcome becomes available. Retain all original target
classes. Select a justified eligible training cohort in Block C; review
death/hospice discharge dispositions before modeling readmission.
Use patient-disjoint splits; identifiers and targets cannot be predictors.
Discharge-time fields support a discharge-stage model, not admission-time
prediction. Fit imputers/encoders/scalers on training data only.

Clustering: the expanded real discharge data may support case-mix/resource
segmentation. Select a documented common cohort and features in Block G;
inspect missing costs, redactions, top-coded stays and source effects before
fitting. Compare the four algorithms on the same prepared records and features.
Do not mix source-specific diagnosis codes into numeric distances.

QUALITY AND PROVENANCE

- Raw page and processed partition SHA-256 hashes and row-count reconciliation.
- Unique source-qualified encounter IDs across the entire acquisition.
- Explicit Parquet schema including nullable source-specific fields.
- Audit missing/invalid duration and money values, age mappings, facility

- Assertions against fabricated dates, facilities, patient links, or labels.
