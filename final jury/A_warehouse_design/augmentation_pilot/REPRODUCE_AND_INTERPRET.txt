SYNTHETIC AUGMENTATION PILOT - REPRODUCTION AND INTERPRETATION

Run from repository root using the existing environment, without upgrading
that environment's libraries:
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install --no-deps --target "final jury\.vendor" imbalanced-learn==0.14.2 sklearn-compat==0.1.6
  .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe "final jury\tools\augmentation_pilot.py"

Alternatively, create a separate environment inside final jury and install
requirements-augmentation.txt. The pilot requires the previously prepared
data/processed/uci_supervised.parquet. No old jury modules/models are imported.

Method documentation:
https://imbalanced-learn.org/stable/over_sampling.html
https://imbalanced-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html

Synthetic minority oversampling with numeric and categorical features
(SMOTENC) was selected for a bounded feasibility experiment. Numeric distance
is scaled using real training data, categorical domains are preserved,
generated counts are rounded to valid integer ranges, and the target class
is assigned by the oversampling procedure. Synthetic outcomes are not observed
readmissions. These checks do not establish clinical plausibility or privacy.

All three variants use the same real-patient split, real-only fitted
preprocessing, features, Random Forest settings, and seed. Only weighting
or addition of synthetic training examples differs. The validation macro F1
selects the variant before the real test set is evaluated. Both the
prespecified real-only baseline and the selected variant are evaluated once.

The baseline here is a new unweighted pilot Random Forest. Its scores are not
the previous Jury 2 model's scores. Different eligibility rules, split,
features, and settings prevent claiming a matched improvement over that model.

Actual findings:
- Training: 59,565 real encounters plus 34,815 generated rows = 94,380.
- Validation: 19,917 real encounters; test: 19,861 real encounters.
- No patient is shared across the three real partitions.
- Validation macro F1: real-only 0.3655; SMOTENC 0.4071; weighted 0.4308.
- Validation <30 recall: real-only 0.0000; SMOTENC 0.3678; weighted 0.2865.
- Class weighting won the prespecified macro-F1 comparison. SMOTENC had
  higher minority recall but lower macro F1 than weighting.
- On real test data, weighting versus the pilot baseline improved macro F1
  from 0.3709 to 0.4382; accuracy fell from 0.5775 to 0.5212.
- The SMOTENC model was not selected and was not tested on the held-out set.
- Of the generated rows, 1,329 exactly match a training feature/label vector.
  Oversampling is not a claim of independent new patient evidence. Similarity
  diagnostics and exact-match counts are in results.json; they do not prove
  that the generated rows are clinically valid.

Do not generate labels for SPARCS and treat them as truth, merge generated
patients into test/validation sets, or claim that increased synthetic volume
is a new independently observed hospital cohort. Whether synthetic data meet
the faculty's expansion requirement remains separate from their ML utility.

Generated and merged training files are under data/processed/augmentation_pilot
and ignored by Git. results.json, validation_comparison.csv, PILOT_SUMMARY.txt,
and artifact_verification.json provide the recorded experimental evidence.
This exploratory experiment does not complete the official later rubric blocks.
